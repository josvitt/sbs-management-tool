A fix for the **IP access lists** panel, and the **SNMP configuration** panel now looks and works like the other panels.

## Fixed
- **Access lists on the switch now show in the panel.** The panel read the lists from the switch but lost the part of each answer that says which list and which field it is, so it showed no lists at all. Every list and rule now appears again.
- **Creating a new access list works again.** Because the panel could not see the lists already on the switch, a new list was given a number that was already taken, and the switch refused it with "WrongValue". New lists now get a free number. The **ACL** tab in a port's or VLAN's details lists your access lists again too.
- **Deleting a notification recipient or a malformed target row shows its progress.** The button spins until the row is gone. Before, nothing happened on screen until the panel was reloaded.

## SNMP configuration: what changes
- **Looks like the other panels.** Each section (recipients, communities, groups, users) has an amber heading with a count. **+ Add recipient**, **+ Add community** and **+ Add group** sit under their headings, above the table.
- **Updates in place.** After a load or a change, only the rows that changed move. The panel is no longer cleared and rebuilt.
- **Refresh does what it says.** The panel's Refresh button re-reads the SNMP configuration. Before, it re-read the rest of the switch and not this panel.
- **"Waiting for data…"** shows while the panel loads for the first time, as in the other panels.
- **Forms open in the standard window.** Adding a recipient, community or group, and editing a community or group, open in the same window the rest of the app uses. **Esc** closes it, and there is no separate Cancel button.
- **One tap to add or save.** Adding and saving act on the first tap. Every delete still asks for a second tap.
- **Errors show under the button** as a red line, like the other forms.
- **Faster forms.** The forms use the community and view lists the panel already loaded instead of reading them from the switch each time they open.
- **Group views are easier to set.** Each security level has its own row of Read, Write and Notify pickers, wide enough to read the view names.
