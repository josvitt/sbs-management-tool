The app is easier to read: values show as words instead of SNMP codes, warnings have their own color, and buttons follow one color rule. Temperatures are also right now.

## Easier to read
- **Words, not codes.** Values such as port state, PoE state and route type show as words ("up", "searching", "remote") instead of "up (1)". Hover a value to see the code the switch sent.
- **Status colors mean one thing.** Green is good, red is a problem, yellow is a warning, and grey is idle. A port with PoE turned on but nothing plugged in now shows grey instead of a warning.
- **Warnings have their own color.** Warnings were the same amber as headings, so they were easy to miss. They are now a separate yellow.
- **Buttons follow one rule.** Green applies or saves, blue is for secondary actions such as Cancel and Copy, and red is for anything that deletes, clears or resets.
- **Calmer forms.** Field labels are in normal case, section headings share one style, and checkboxes match the app's colors.
- **Clearer empty screens.** The MIB Explorer says what to do before you search.
- **Easier to use with a keyboard.** Every control shows a clear outline when you tab to it, and animations stop if your computer is set to reduce motion.

## Fixed
- **Temperatures were ten times too low.** The C1300 reports whole degrees, and the app divided them by ten, so 54 °C showed as 5.4 °C.
- **The serial port error is plain English.** If serial support isn't installed, the app says how to add it instead of showing a program error.

## Upgrading
Nothing to do. Install the update as usual.
