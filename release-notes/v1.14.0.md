A new **Switching security** panel turns on and adjusts the switch's protections against rogue DHCP servers, ARP spoofing and multicast floods.

## New: multicast snooping
- Turn **IGMP snooping** (IPv4) and **MLD snooping** (IPv6) on or off for the switch and for each VLAN, so multicast such as video only goes to the ports that asked for it.
- Set the **IGMP snooping querier** for a VLAN: on or off, its address, IGMP version 2 or 3, and whether it takes part in querier election.
- Turn **immediate leave** on for a VLAN.
- See the groups the switch has heard and its router ports.

## New: DHCP snooping
- Turn DHCP snooping on or off for the switch and for each VLAN.
- Choose whether the switch checks the client's MAC address and keeps its bindings in a file.
- See the addresses the switch has seen handed out.

## New: ARP inspection
- Turn ARP inspection on or off for the switch and for each VLAN, and see how many ARP packets were forwarded and dropped.
- Make **ARP lists** of devices with a fixed address and assign a list to a VLAN.
- Turn the extra packet check on or off, and set how often it logs.

## New: Security tab on each port
A port's popout has a **Security** tab to mark the port as trusted for DHCP snooping and ARP inspection. Trust the ports toward your DHCP server and your other switches.

## Not included
Adding a fixed DHCP snooping binding by hand.

## Upgrading
Nothing to do. Install the update as usual.
